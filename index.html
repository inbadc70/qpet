You don't need a bigger team. You need to learn how to give Claude the work: your instructions, your files, your tools. Here's what that looks like.

01

Prompt & AI Fundamentals:


Covers how large language models (LLMs) work, basic terminology like tokens and context windows, and why precise instructions matter.

02

Structure & Frameworks:


Teaches formulas like RACI (Role, Action, Context, Input) or the BRIDGE framework to build consistent, structured prompts

03

Best Practices:


Explores workspace features like custom GPTs, stored memories, project guidelines, and data privacy safeguards

Advanced Techniques:


Focuses on methods like Chain-of-Thought (guiding AI step-by-step reasoning), few-shot prompting (using examples), and XML tagging for structured outputs.

Practical Workflows & Projects:


Applies skills to real-world tasks such as data analysis, content creation, image generation, or customer feedback classification.
